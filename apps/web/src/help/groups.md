# Group sets

## What this screen is

A group set splits the students of a classroom into groups. It belongs to
the classroom, not to a project: several projects can follow the same set,
and you form the groups once. **New group set** creates one.

## Forming the groups

- **By hand**: **New group**, then drag a student from *No group* into a
  group. With the keyboard: Space to pick up, arrows to move, Space to drop.
  **Undo** takes a move back.
- **At random**: **Form at random** cuts the students in no group into
  groups of the size you choose, which differ by at most one. You pick
  whether the remainder goes to smaller or to larger groups. Groups already
  formed are not touched.
- **By the students**: **Open to students…** sets a maximum size and a date.
  Until then they create, join and leave groups themselves; you keep every
  right. At the closing nothing happens by itself: you place the rest.

The **Maximum size** only flags a group above it, unless the set is open to
students, where it binds them.

## Once repositories exist

A group that has a repository cannot be deleted, and a set a project uses
cannot be deleted either. Moving a student out of such a group changes who
reaches the repository: Quiz lists who loses and who gets access, and asks
you to **Confirm the move**. GitHub follows in the background; a revocation
GitHub refuses is retried, and the repository shows *access to revoke*.

On a project's page, **Resync with the set** brings back in step a copy
that stopped following the set at the deadline.
