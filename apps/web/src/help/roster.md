# Roster

## What this list is

The class list. Each row is a seat: **pending** until the student signs in
with a matching e-mail, **claimed** once attached to their account.
A *staff* badge marks a teacher seat.

## How claiming works

Students claim their seat automatically at their first sign-in — no
invitation code. Matching runs against every address the identity provider
reveals, not only the one they signed in with. If an entry is ambiguous (the
same student on two seats, or an address shared by two accounts) it is
flagged **conflict** for you to resolve: nothing is attached on a guess.

## Extra time

The **Extra time** column is the accommodation, in percent of the nominal
duration of every timed evaluation. It comes from the import file when the
sheet carries a column for it, and can be edited row by row.

## How to manage rows

Edit a row to fix a name, an address or the extra time. Changing the address
revokes the claim: the seat goes back to pending and the holder of the new
address claims it. Removing a student takes them off the list; their past
results are kept.
