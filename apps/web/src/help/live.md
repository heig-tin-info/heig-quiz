# Live dashboard

## Before the start

While the evaluation is in the waiting room, the grid gives way to a ring:
how many are present out of how many are enrolled, who is here, who is not
yet, and who has extra time. Nothing starts until you press **Start**.

## The grid

Students down, questions across. The identity column stays put while the
question columns scroll; it carries the score, the individual time left and
a presence dot. A cell is blank, in progress, answered, and after grading
correct, partial or wrong — the legend under the grid names all six.

Three switches hide what you do not want on a projector: **Names**,
**Answers**, **Results**. With the names off, each row keeps a stable
pseudonym.

## Controls

**Pause** freezes every countdown; **Extend** adds 1, 5 or 10 minutes to
the whole class. A row's menu extends one student by 5 minutes, or closes
and reopens that one attempt; reopening only works while the evaluation is
running or paused. **Close** ends it for everybody and asks first. Once it
is closed, the only thing left here is going to the grading.

## Inspect

Click a cell to read what the student wrote, beside the grid — never over
it. The arrows move to the next student or the next question.

## Shortcuts

`N`, `R`, `S` flip the three switches, `Space` pauses and resumes, `F` goes
full screen, `Esc` closes the panel. The countdowns follow the server's
clock, and the page updates itself without reloading.
