# My classrooms

## What this page shows

One card per classroom you belong to: its name, the course it follows, the
period and the teaching staff. Evaluations appear here once your teachers
open them.

## How you got here

Your teacher adds your school e-mail address to the class list. The seat is
attached to your account the first time you sign in with a matching address
— there is no code to enter and nothing to accept.

If a classroom you expect is missing, check that you signed in with (or have
registered at the identity provider) the address your teacher used.

## Extra time

If an accommodation was granted to you, the card shows it. It applies
automatically to every timed evaluation of that classroom.

## Who else can see this page

To help you with a problem you report, an administrator of the platform can
open a view of your account as you see it. It lasts at most an hour, it
cannot answer or submit anything in your place, and each one is recorded in
the audit log under the administrator's name.
