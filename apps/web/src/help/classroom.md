# Classroom

## What this screen is

One classroom: a group following a course for one period. It holds a roster
and the evaluations you run with that group. The eyebrow above the title
goes back to the course.

## Roster

One row per seat: **pending** until a student signs in with a matching
address, **claimed** once attached to their account, **conflict** when the
matching is ambiguous. The **Extra time** column is the accommodation, in
percent of the nominal duration of every timed evaluation. **Add students**
imports an Excel or CSV export.

**Join as student** gives you a seat in your own classroom, so you can take
the evaluation the way a student does. That seat is flagged *staff* and
stays out of the headcount. From an evaluation, **View as student** does the
whole thing in one click — it offers the seat if you have none, then walks
you through the real waiting room and player.

## Evaluations

The evaluations of this classroom, each with its state — draft, scheduled,
waiting room, running, paused, closed, grading, released. From a row you
reach its configuration, the live dashboard, the grading panel or the
results, depending on where it stands. **New evaluation**, at the top right
while this tab is open, creates one.

## Settings

A click on the period (or on **Set period** when there is none) opens its
dates and its label. The **Settings** tab holds the rest: **Rename**, the
**Drill** switch, the **GitHub** connection when the platform has one, and
the classroom's end.

**Connect to GitHub** picks an organization where Quiz's App is installed,
or installs it on another one; the checks then say whether the App sees
every repository, the organization's plan, and the LLM secret.
**Disconnect** deletes nothing on GitHub.

**Archive** puts a finished classroom aside without deleting anything;
**Restore** brings it back. **Delete** is the other thing entirely: the
roster, the evaluations, the attempts, the answers and their gradings go,
after a confirmation naming the classroom. The questions of the pools are
never touched.
