# Forming your group

## What you can do

While your teacher keeps the groups open, until the date shown:

- **Create a group**: you join it at once. The name is optional.
- **Join** a group that is not full.
- **Leave** your group, or **Rename** it.

## What you see

The groups with their members' names and sizes, and the students who have no
group yet. Nothing else about them.

## When it ends

On the date, the groups close and your teacher places whoever has no group.
Once a group of the set gets its repository, the groups can no longer change.

## Group projects

One member accepts the project and the repository belongs to the group. Every
member must link their own GitHub account first.
