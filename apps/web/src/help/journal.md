# Journal

## What this screen is

The classroom's course documentation, read in the app. It is not an
activity: no grade, no deadline. Students read it from this tab; you create
it, or remove it, in the classroom's **Settings**.

## Where the pages live

- **In Quiz**: written here with **Edit**, in the same editor as question
  statements. Every save is a revision: **History** lists them and restores
  one. **Pages** adds a page or deletes one, and **Deleted pages** brings
  one back.
- **In a GitHub repository**: written in your own tools, and Quiz shows a
  read-only copy. **Edit on GitHub** opens the file; a push updates the copy
  by itself, and **Refresh** reads it again now.

## What a page can say

The page's settings hold a **Title**, a **Date**, a **Draft** switch (staff
only) and **Visible to students from**: the page stays hidden until then and
appears without a reload. Students never see a draft or a page not yet
visible, nor that one exists. Raw HTML is shown as text; an image from
outside the journal is dropped, with a warning on the page.

If a colleague saved a page while you were editing, nothing is merged: your
text stays in the editor, and **Copy my text** lets you redo your changes on
the new version.
