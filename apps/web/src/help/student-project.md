# My project

## The steps

1. **Link GitHub**, once. You come back to this page.
2. **Accept**: your private repository is created, in up to a minute.
3. **Open the invitation**: accept it on GitHub, once. Lost it? **Resend the
   invitation**.
4. **Open repository**, then clone it, work and push.

The page shows one button at a time, the next step.

## Your score

The score under **Score** comes from the latest commit that was graded. It is
**indicative**: it changes with every push and is not your grade. **No score
yet** means no commit of yours was graded.

## Protected files

Some files (the grading workflow, the instructions) are protected. If a push
changes one, a commit puts the teacher's version back. Your work stays; your
change to that file does not count.

## Updates from your teacher

An update arrives as a pull request on GitHub. Open it, resolve the
conflicts and merge it yourself.

## The deadline

At the deadline, if your teacher locked the project, your repository becomes
read-only; otherwise it stays open, but pushes after the deadline do not
count. The score is frozen on the **evaluated commit**; a grading run started
in time may still finish. The clock is the server's, not your computer's.

## The release

Once your teacher releases the scores, the page shows your final score, your
grade and the teacher's comment. You get a notification.
