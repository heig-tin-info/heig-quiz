# The training course

## What this screen is

Your own training course: one classroom, no students, two quizzes made
for you, and four missions on the real screens of the platform. Nothing
here is graded: what you do as a student counts in no statistic and no
practice deck.

The card above the activities shows each mission's state. It is read from
what you have actually done, never ticked by hand, and its button leads to
where the mission happens — it never does the gesture for you.

## 1. Be a student

Click **Join as student** on this page: you get a seat in your own
classroom, badged *staff*. Then open your account menu and **Switch to
student view**: the portal becomes what a student sees. Take the welcome
quiz: one question per type, each about a gesture of the platform, with an
explanation that says what you would configure to obtain that behaviour.
Switch back the same way.

## 2. Run a class

Open the live quiz, a draft in Live mode. Walk its three screens and the
start checklist with the waiting-room preview. Open the room, open a second
tab in the student view (the view is per tab), start, answer, close. Grade
the free-text and the diagram questions by hand, release the grades, then
read them from the student view.

## 3. Make it yours

On the pools page, **New pool**. On the training course's page, **Link a
pool**. In your pool, create a question with the wand, try it, publish it.
Save the welcome quiz as a template, instantiate it, add your question, then
edit the question and watch the "newer version" badge appear. Preview the
quiz, take it from the seat of mission 1, reset your own attempt.

## 4. The tools around (optional)

Ask the assistant "how do I give a student extra time?" and let it open the
roster. In Settings, create a personal token or connect Claude through
MCP, create a question from there and find it in your pool. This mission
reads "Not available on this server" where neither the assistant nor the
connections are configured.

## Starting over

The training course is an ordinary course: deleting it from its Settings
and starting the training again gives you a fresh one. A pool you made in
mission 3 is yours and stays.
