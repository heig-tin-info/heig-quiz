# Courses

## What a course is

A course is the lasting unit you teach — "Programmation C" — with a name and
a short code. It outlives a class: the same course carries the classrooms of
2026, then those of 2027.

One card per course. Its classrooms are listed with their period and their
headcount; a click opens one.

## Classrooms

**New classroom** asks for a name and, optionally, a period. A classroom is
one group following this course for one period, and it is where rosters,
evaluations and results live.

## Staff

**Add a staff member** takes the e-mail of an account that has already
signed in once. Every member of the staff has the same rights on the
course's classrooms — there is no owner. The overflow menu also removes
someone from the staff.

## Pools of this course

The evaluations of a course draw their questions from the pools linked to
it. **Link a pool** adds one; **Unlink from this course** removes the link
and nothing else — the pool and its questions stay where they are. A pool
that is not linked to any course is still yours, it is simply not offered
when you fill an evaluation.

## Deleting

Deleting a course takes its classrooms, their rosters and their results with
it, after a confirmation naming the course. Its pools are not touched.
