| 20 | Schematic question: how is a circuit graded, given that netlist comparison is out of scope (00 §0.6)? | **Settled (ADR-019)**: by SIMULATION of the output waveform, not by topology. The student's schematic and the teacher's reference are each rebuilt into a SPICE netlist server-side, run through ngspice (`apps/runner`, language `spice`) under every stimulus, and `v(out)` is compared by the rule of the stimulus's analysis: a transient passes when the normalised RMS distance is at or under `tolerance` times the reference's swing; an AC sweep (ADR-040) when its Bode plot stays inside an envelope around the reference's (`grading.bode`: gain within `magDb` above a floor `floorDb` under the reference's peak, phase within `phaseDeg`, one-sided below the floor). A circuit drawn differently that behaves the same is correct; an isomorphism check would need canonical forms for series/parallel merges and would still say no to it. The reference is simulated at grading time, never cached in the config. |
