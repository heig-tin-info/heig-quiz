# 6. Open questions

Only unresolved or partly settled decisions are listed here. Keep their original
numbers: a missing number is in [settled questions](history/settled-questions.md),
which preserves the recorded answers and their rationale. Do not reuse numbers.
An implemented assumption does not by itself settle a product decision.

The unmarked original questions 3 and 6 remain here: their current assumptions
are implemented, but their rows contained no explicit settlement. Question 27
keeps its unresolved student-breakdown part; question 43 keeps its deferred audit.

| 21 | Restoring a soft-deleted question: does it exist, or should the filter go? F-QST-11 specifies the soft delete (the question leaves the pool, its published versions stay readable) and requires no way back. Yet the pool screen offers a "Deleted" filter (`apps/web/src/pool/QuestionSearchBar.tsx`, `includeDeleted` in `pool/filters.ts`) that lists deleted questions with nothing a teacher can do to them: no restore route, and, since the audit of 2026-09-22 (B-11), no `restoreQuestion` service either — it was deleted as speculative, having never had a caller. | **Open.** Assumed for now: the filter stays, as the only way to see that a question was deleted rather than lost, and the pool keeps no restore. The two coherent endings are a `POST /questions/:id/restore` with the button the filter is missing, or dropping the filter and leaving a deleted question visible only through its frozen versions. Whichever is chosen, it settles both halves at once. |
| 22 | Code question: which wall-clock budget judges a VISIBLE case outside the grade? Since the audit of 2026-09-22 (R-06) the grade, the run route (`POST /attempts/:id/run`), the player and the editor's "try" share one rule (`caseVerdict` in `packages/qt-code`), comparison options included, except for time: the grade re-applies each case's own `timeMs` to the measured time, while the other three count only the runner's own `timedOut`, which is measured against the request's single, global budget. `CodeStudent` carries `limits.timeMs` but no per-case budget. The scenario is the one `packages/qt-code/src/grade.test.ts` pins ("times a case out on its own budget"): `limits.timeMs = 2000`, a case with `timeMs: 200` that runs in 900 ms reads "Passed" in the player and "Timed out" (0 points) in the grade — the residue of the ADR-015 §2 gap. | **Open.** Assumed for now: the player and the run route stay on the runner's `timedOut`, because a browser run's timing is not evidence (ADR-015) and the per-case budget is not published. The two coherent endings: carry each visible case's effective `timeMs` in `CodeStudent` and pass it to `caseVerdict` in the run route (and, if a browser timing is accepted as indicative, in the player), or drop per-case budgets altogether so the one global `limits.timeMs` is the only budget everywhere. Either one settles the three sites at once. |
| 23 | Grading queue: paginate `GET /evaluations/:id/grading` before a class of 100 meets it? Today `gradingQueue` (`apps/api/src/modules/grading/service.ts`) answers every cell of the selection in one response — for 100 students × 20 items, 2 000 entries, each carrying its `studentView`, its `solutionView` and its grading history (superseded rows included), built before the `state` filter is applied. The audit of 2026-09-22 (D-12) measured the cost and proposed keyset pagination; it is a contract change (`packages/contracts`). Since ADR-044 the panel reads one question at a time (`?itemId=`), so a response holds one item's answers — 100 entries for a class of 100 — and the grading table sorts and filters them in the browser, which pagination would have to take over. | **Open, deferred** (campaign supervisor, 2026-09-23). Assumed for now: one unpaginated response, which holds for the classes of today (≤ 30 students). The coherent ending is keyset pagination on (item position, attempt) with the counts computed separately, so the panel's "validated / proposed" totals stay those of the whole selection; to be taken up before the first class of about 100 students is graded on the platform. |

| 27 | Do the grading criteria of a question (an essay's rubric, a short answer's `llm` rubric) reach a student when the evaluation shows the key (`showKey`, an exercise with immediate feedback included)? | **Settled (ADR-037, decided by the teacher on 2026-09-29)**: no, they are teacher-only. The key a student reads goes through the type's `studentSolution` hook in `studentSolutionView` (`apps/api/src/modules/live/studentView.ts`): an essay keeps its model answer (as the expected answer); the teacher's surfaces keep everything. A circuit's key is its reference alone, for everyone, and an `llm` matcher is refused at publication. Whether a student will read a per-criterion LLM breakdown in the grading details (04 §4.8, "Scoring, later") stays open. For now an LLM's justification is kept teacher-only: it lives in the grading's details, never in the comment, and is stripped from every student payload whatever the policy (ADR-045). *Amendment (ADR-063, 2026-10-02): a teacher may copy the justification into the comment by hand, from the correction form, and edit it before saving; nothing else carries it to a student, and the per-criterion breakdown (`details.ai`) stays teacher-only.* |
