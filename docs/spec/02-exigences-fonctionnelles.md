# 2. Functional requirements

Every requirement is identified `F-AREA-nn`, with its phase P1 / P2 / P3 and its priority M / S / C. A requirement must be verifiable by a test or a demonstration.

## F-AUTH Authentication and accounts

| Id | Requirement | Phase | Prio |
|---|---|---|---|
| F-AUTH-01 | The user signs in through edu-ID with OpenID Connect. No local password. | P1 | M |
| F-AUTH-02 | On first sign-in, the account is created with name, email and a role derived from the edu-ID affiliation: a `staff` affiliation scoped to one of our institutions (`STAFF_AFFILIATION_DOMAINS`) gives `teacher`, unless a `student` affiliation of one of those institutions blocks it (a student elsewhere does not); anything else — another institution's `staff`, an unscoped `staff` — gives `student`. The default lists `heig-vd.ch` and `hes-so.ch`, because edu-ID releases HEIG-VD staff as `staff@hes-so.ch`; as an accepted consequence, the staff of the other HES-SO schools qualify too. The role is recomputed at every login by the same rule as any later change (`roles.ts`). | P1 | M |
| F-AUTH-03 | The admin is identified by a list of edu-ID identifiers in the configuration. They may promote or demote a user to `teacher`. | P1 | M |
| F-AUTH-04 | The session persists for 30 days. An explicit sign-out is available. A running exam never asks to sign in again. | P1 | M |
| F-AUTH-05 | A participant without an account may join an evaluation in `poll` mode through a session code, anonymously: no pseudonym is asked for nor stored (see 06 no. 18). Only an anonymous poll, which belongs to no classroom, takes such a participant; a classroom's poll admits its roster and its staff, signed in (ADR-014, addendum 2026-09-27). | P2 | M |

## F-ORG Courses, classrooms, rosters

| Id | Requirement | Phase | Prio |
|---|---|---|---|
| F-ORG-01 | A teacher creates a course with a name and a short code. They may add other teachers to it. | P1 | M |
| F-ORG-02 | A course references one or more pools. The course's evaluations draw from these pools. | P1 | M |
| F-ORG-03 | A teacher creates a classroom in a course with a name and a period: a free label and, optionally, a first and a last month (both or neither, the last not before the first). The form offers the current-or-next semester and the one after as presets and prefills the first; a preset fills the label only while it is empty or still the previous preset's name. The sidebar's flat list of classrooms shows those not archived, of a course the teacher has not hidden (F-ORG-11), whose period covers today (the browser's local date), with one month of margin on each side; an undated classroom always shows. The course cards, the course tree, the palette and the student home list every non-archived classroom. A classroom may be archived: it leaves the course list, the sidebar and the palette, and stays reachable from its course behind "Show archived". | P1 | M |
| F-ORG-04 | The roster is imported from CSV with at least the edu-ID email. Lines already known are merged, never duplicated. | P1 | M |
| F-ORG-05 | A student whose email appears in a roster is attached to the classroom on their first sign-in. | P1 | M |
| F-ORG-06 | Removed (ADR-053): no classroom join code; a student enters a roster only through the teacher (import or manual add) and is matched at sign-in. | — | — |
| F-ORG-07 | Every roster line carries an extra time in percent, 0 by default, and a free note. *It never applies to a project's deadline (D13): a project repository gets an individual deadline from its staff instead (F-PROJ-09, D13 as amended 2026-10-02).* | P1 | M |
| F-ORG-08 | The teacher may remove a student from a roster. Their past attempts are kept. | P1 | M |
| F-ORG-09 | Deleting a classroom deletes its evaluations, attempts, answers and gradings after a confirmation that names the classroom. The questions of the pool and the course's evaluation templates are not affected: a template belongs to the course and survives every classroom (ADR-031). Deleting a course deletes its templates, and its confirmation names how many. The classroom's GitHub link and its journal go with it: a journal in Quiz is deleted with its pages, revisions and assets, and the confirmation says so with the number of pages (when there are pages, the teacher types the classroom's name); for a journal in a GitHub repository only the copy goes, the repository is kept (F-JRN-04, ADR-057). Its projects go too, their rows only: no repository is ever deleted on GitHub, and the confirmation says so (F-PROJ-16, D19). | P1 | M |
| F-ORG-10 | A classroom may be duplicated to a new period, without roster nor attempts, with its evaluations as drafts. The copy's months are shifted by one semester (`shiftSemester` in `packages/domain`): a period that is exactly a semester becomes the next one (autumn 2026 → spring 2027), any other moves six months; an undated classroom stays undated. | P2 | S |
| F-ORG-11 | A teacher (or an admin) may hide a course they reach from their OWN navigation — the course list, both sections of the sidebar and the command palette — and show it again. Hiding is per user: the other staff members see nothing change, and nothing about the course changes either (classrooms, evaluations, pools). Pickers and the MCP `list_courses` keep listing a hidden course, and the course list brings hidden courses back behind "Show hidden" (#155, ADR-032, 06 no. 26). | P1 | S |
| F-ORG-12 | Every course has a **page of its own** (`/courses/:id`), reached from its name on the course list (card and table), from its row in the sidebar's course tree and from the command palette ("Go to the course"). It is in **tabs**, as the classroom's (F-ORG-13), each a path of its own (`/courses/:id/<tab>`): **Classrooms**, the default (the archived ones behind "Show archived"), **Templates** (F-EVAL-19), **Linked pools** (link and unlink), **Members** (the staff, each with "Remove from the staff" while more than one remains) and **Settings**. The Templates tab has an empty state that names the way to create one ("New template", "Save as template" in an evaluation's menu); the course list's card no longer lists templates, so the page is their one surface. The header's one primary action is the open tab's — New classroom, New template, Link a pool, Add a staff member — and Settings has none. Settings holds, in settings rows (D24), the name and the code (a code another course holds is refused, `409 duplicate_code`), hiding the course (F-ORG-11) and deleting it (F-ORG-09), which returns to the course list; the header shows the name and no longer edits it. Every staff member has every right on the course (D04); roles are not part of this requirement (product owner, 2026-10-02). A course the caller does not reach reads as one that does not exist (ADR-031 addendum of 2026-09-28). | P1 | S |
| F-ORG-13 | The teacher's classroom page has a **Settings** tab, in settings rows (D24). It holds what the header held — rename, archive, delete (F-ORG-09) and the drill switch (F-DRILL-04) — plus a **GitHub** section (F-GH-02, F-GH-03) and a **Journal** section (F-JRN-02 to F-JRN-05), available whether the classroom is connected or not (a journal in Quiz needs no GitHub, ADR-057); the header keeps the name and the period. Its one accent is "Connect to GitHub" while the classroom is not connected, and nothing once it is: a classroom connected for its projects is not pushed towards a journal. A classroom that is never connected is a plain Quiz classroom, and nothing else of the page changes. | P2 | M |
| F-ORG-14 | The student's **Courses** entry (sidebar and bottom bar) is a route of its own that lists the student's classrooms — the classrooms where they hold a claimed seat, archived ones excepted — as cards; a card opens the classroom's page (F-ORG-15), and so does every classroom card of the student home (D07). **Activities** stays the summary of the active activities of every classroom, today's home: what is open now and what is coming, the latter grouped by day — Today, Tomorrow, This week, Later — by the calendar day each activity opens, in the browser's time zone ("This week" runs from the day after tomorrow to Sunday, weeks starting on Monday; an opening already passed is Today, an undated one Later), the soonest first in each, an empty day not drawn (product owner, 2026-10-01; no calendar view until the projects bring deadlines, D07). **Grades** (sidebar and bottom bar) is a route of its own, `/grades`: the student's finished work — what the home's "Past" listed, which the home no longer shows — grouped by classroom, the classroom of the newest row first, archived classrooms included and marked; per row the activity, its kind, its date, a status (released, grade not shared — released under the policy `none` —, results available, results pending, handed in, not taken), and the points and the Swiss grade where F-RES-04 lets the student read them; a "results available" row shows its points marked indicative, and no grade. A row opens the feedback page where it has something to show. No average there unless the teacher publishes the classroom's mean (F-GBOOK-05). A project row shows under its classroom like an evaluation's once its scores are released (F-PROJ-14). | P2 | M |
| F-ORG-15 | A student has a **page per classroom** at `/classrooms/:id`, the same address as the teacher's, which opens the student's page for a student, for a teacher in the student view (ADR-018) and in an impersonation session (ADR-034). Header: the classroom's name, the course's code and name, the period, the teachers, the student's time bonus. Its tabs are routes: **Activities** (by default) in three groups, Open now, Upcoming (grouped by day as on the home, F-ORG-14), Past, whose one accent is the button of the single most urgent open activity; **Journal** only when the classroom has one (F-JRN-07); **Grades**, the student's own cells of the classroom's gradebook (F-GBOOK-05). A published project is listed in Activities like an evaluation, in the group its dates put it in (F-PROJ-04), and counts for the most urgent accent by its deadline. Empty state: nothing to do in this classroom right now. An archived classroom's page stays reachable by its address, as it is. A classroom where the caller is neither on the course's staff nor holding a claimed seat reads as one that does not exist (404). | P2 | M |

|---|---|---|---|
| F-POOL-01 | Every teacher has a private pool created automatically and may create others. The personal pool (`Polls`) is created on first use, by the first question the teacher keeps after a live poll (ADR-014, addenda item 6); a teacher who never keeps one never sees it. | P1 | M |
| F-POOL-02 | A pool contains hierarchical categories and questions. A question is in a single category or at the root. | P1 | M |
| F-POOL-03 | The list of questions can be filtered by type, tags, difficulty, category, free text on the internal name and the statement. | P1 | M |
| F-POOL-04 | A question may be copied to another pool. The copy references its origin. | P1 | S |
| F-POOL-05 | A pool may be shared with other teachers with a `reader`, `contributor` or `owner` role. | P2 | S |
| F-POOL-06 | A global public pool is readable by every teacher. The admin designates its contributors. | P2 | S |
| F-POOL-07 | A pool exports as an archive of YAML files in the canonical format, one file per question, the category tree as folders. Importing this archive recreates the pool or merges into an existing pool by id. | P1 | S |
| F-POOL-08 | Import of GIFT and Moodle XML files for the supported types. Questions that cannot be converted are listed with the reason. | P2 | C |
| F-POOL-09 | Deleting a pool is refused while an evaluation or an evaluation template pins one of its question versions; the refusal names the holders the caller can open and counts the others (ADR-031). | P1 | M |
| F-POOL-10 | A teacher stars questions of any pool they can see — a reader and a public pool included — as personal favourites: a colleague never sees nor clears them, and nothing is audited. On the pool screen, a star on each row and card, Space on the focused one, "Star" in the bulk bar and "Clear favourites" (this pool, after a confirm that counts them). In the question picker of an evaluation or a template, the starred questions of the shown pool come first, and "Add favourites" adds every one that can be added, says what it skipped and why, then offers to unstar them. A star follows its question when it is moved to another pool; a copy starts without one; a soft-deleted question's star stays hidden until it is restored (ADR-040). | P1 | S |

## F-EVAL Configuring an evaluation

| Id | Requirement | Phase | Prio |
|---|---|---|---|
| F-EVAL-01 | The teacher creates an evaluation in a classroom with a title, a mode `exam`, `exercise` or `poll`, and an ordered list of questions taken from the course's pools. | P1 | M |
| F-EVAL-02 | Every item carries a number of points. By default: 1 for the simple types, the number of test cases for code, the number of blanks for cloze. | P1 | M |
| F-EVAL-03 | Adding a question freezes its current published version. The evaluation shows when a more recent version exists and offers "Update" item by item or globally, as long as no attempt exists. | P1 | M |
| F-EVAL-04 | Time settings: `duration` per-student duration from their start, `deadline` common end time, `manual` closed by the teacher. `exam` requires `duration` or `deadline`. `exercise` has a due date. | P1 | M |
| F-EVAL-05 | The roster's extra time applies to `duration`. In `deadline` mode it extends the individual end beyond the common end, by a share of the **announced** window (`closes_at − opens_at` as the teacher set it, decision D8): time added to everybody during the run — an extension to all, a pause — moves the common end and never grows that share, for a late starter or a reopened attempt alike. The announced window follows the teacher's timing edits while the configuration is editable, and is frozen once it locks (F-EVAL-03) (#253). | P1 | M |
| F-EVAL-06 | Waiting room: `skip` the student starts alone, `auto` start when everybody present is there, `manual` the teacher starts. | P1 | M |
| F-EVAL-07 | Navigation: `free`, `forward_only`, `milestones` with a flag per item beyond which there is no going back. | P1 | M |
| F-EVAL-08 | Presentation: `zen` one question per screen, `continuous` scrolling, `student_choice` only with `free` navigation. | P1 | M |
| F-EVAL-09 | Shuffling: question order yes / no, choice order yes / no for shuffleable questions. The order is derived from the attempt's seed and stable for a student. | P1 | M |
| F-EVAL-10 | Grade scale: grade = 1 + 5 × points / total, capped at 6, rounded to the tenth, configurable method: nearest by default. An item may be marked **bonus** in the builder: its points are left out of the total, so they can only lift a student (a score may read "21 / 18"); under negative marking its score is floored at 0; the flag is locked like the points, and an exam or exercise whose total is 0 is not opened (`no_graded_points`). The `threshold` scale is gone (ADR-052). | P1 | M |
| F-EVAL-11 | Feedback: `none`, `on_release` after the teacher releases the results, `immediate` after the student validates a question, reserved to the `exercise` and `poll` modes. The feedback includes or not the answer key and the explanation, two distinct options. A correction published while an exercise runs counts as the release for `on_release` (F-EVAL-27). | P1 | M |
| F-EVAL-12 | An evaluation may restrict IP addresses to a list of prefixes. The access code a student used to type is removed: a code on the board proves no presence ([ADR-053](../adr/ADR-053-retrait-des-codes-d-entree.md)); `access_code` is a poll's session code only (ADR-014). | P1 | S |
| F-EVAL-13 | An evaluation may enable the recommended full-screen mode and the logging of tab visibility changes. Nothing is blocking, everything is visible to the teacher. | P2 | S |
| F-EVAL-14 | An evaluation can be duplicated to the same classroom or to another one, provided the target course links the pools of all its questions (F-EVAL-01). | P1 | S |
| F-EVAL-15 | An `exercise` evaluation may allow several attempts, keeping the best or the last one (the teacher's choice), with an optional maximum. A retake starts blank, with a new question order, once the previous attempt is handed in and while the evaluation is open; the student sees the score of each attempt, not the correction, until the evaluation closes — or until the teacher publishes the correction (F-EVAL-27). Results (their per-question statistics included), grade and CSV use the kept attempt; the pool's question statistics count exams only (F-STAT-01). An exam keeps one attempt (ADR-025). | P2 | C |
| F-EVAL-16 | An `exam` or `exercise` evaluation may use **negative marking**: every choice question is then scored so that a wrong answer costs points (single answer: +1 or −1/(n − 1); several: c/C − w/W, not floored) and no answer costs nothing. Per-question points may be negative; the evaluation total is floored at 0 and the grade computed from it. The student is told in the waiting room and on each choice question. Frozen once an attempt exists (ADR-026, issue #130). | P1 | S |
| F-EVAL-17 | An `exam` may require **Safe Exam Browser**. The student's card then downloads a one-time `.seb` that opens the exam in SEB under their identity, with no second sign-in; the session so opened reaches that exam only, and a portal session cannot sit the exam (ADR-027, issue #139). While sat in SEB, every request of the session carries SEB's Config Key header, checked against the session's own file (ADR-051 §3). | P2 | S |
| F-EVAL-18 | An `exam` or `exercise` evaluation may be **saved as a template** of its course. The template keeps the items (frozen versions, points, order, milestones, bonus flags), the settings, the grade scale, the feedback policy, the MCQ policy and the duration, and drops the opening and closing dates and the IP list. A poll cannot be a template (`422`). The template starts at revision 1, and the source evaluation is linked to it at that revision, as an instance of it (F-EVAL-26 then applies); a source that was already an instance of another template is relinked, and the origin it loses is recorded in the `template.create` audit entry (`previousOrigin`). Offered in the evaluation's menu and in its row menu of the classroom's list (ADR-031, issue #151; addendum of 2026-09-30). | P2 | S |
| F-EVAL-19 | The course lists its templates to every member of its staff, with their mode, number of questions, points and revision; anyone else gets the 404 of a missing template. A template is never opened, answered, graded, nor listed among a classroom's evaluations. | P2 | S |
| F-EVAL-20 | **Instantiate** makes a draft evaluation in a classroom of the SAME course from a template, recording the template and its revision. A question whose pool is no longer linked to the course blocks it with the list of such items; a deprecated version only warns. An instance with `deadline` timing is born without dates and cannot be opened until they are filled (F-EVAL-04). | P2 | S |
| F-EVAL-21 | Deleting a template leaves its instances untouched; they only lose their link to it. | P2 | S |
| F-EVAL-22 | At evaluation creation, "Start from a template" is offered only when the course has at least one template; otherwise the creation dialog is unchanged (08). | P2 | S |
| F-EVAL-23 | The last configuration step is a **pre-flight checklist**: what the API would refuse (no question, incomplete timing) blocks the launch; stale question versions, a template that moved since (F-EVAL-26), an empty roster, roster conflicts and a common end already past are warnings, each with its fix; the rest of the rules is shown for information. From there the teacher opens the waiting room now — the evaluation itself when it has none (`lobby: skip`) — or **schedules** the opening: a scheduled evaluation carries an opening time (`opensAt`), the move to `scheduled` is refused without one, and the ticker opens it at that time (issue #152). | P1 | M |
| F-EVAL-24 | From the course page (F-ORG-12), a member of the course's staff **creates an empty template**: a title, a mode (`exam` or `exercise`) and a preset, exactly as for an evaluation's creation but in the course. A `poll` is refused (`422 template_poll`). The template starts at revision 1 with no question, and is filled in place (F-EVAL-25). Audited as `template.create`, without `from` (ADR-031 addendum of 2026-09-28). | P2 | S |
| F-EVAL-25 | A template is **edited in place** by any member of its course's staff, through routes of its own (`/templates/:id/...`), never through an evaluation's: its title; its items (add from the course's linked pools only, remove, reorder, points, milestones, update to the latest published version); its settings, grade scale, feedback policy, MCQ policy and duration, under the same rules as an untouched draft. Nothing of a run is editable: a patch carrying `opensAt`, `closesAt`, `ipAllowlist`, `accessCode` (which no evaluation has any more, [ADR-053](../adr/ADR-053-retrait-des-codes-d-entree.md)) or any unknown top-level field is a `400` (an unknown key inside `settings` or `feedbackPolicy` is dropped, as on an evaluation). Every committed request that changes the content (anything but the title) moves the **revision** by exactly one — "update all to the latest version" included — and a title-only or no-op request does not; each write is audited as `template.update` with what changed and the revision. The editor shows, per item, a newer published version (stale), a deprecated version (which *Instantiate* warns about) and a pool no longer linked to the course (which blocks it). *Instantiate* copies the template under a lock, so the revision it records is the one it copied. Instances are never changed by an edit; they take a newer revision only by a pull (F-EVAL-26). | P2 | S |
| F-EVAL-26 | An instance whose template has moved (its `origin_revision` below the template's `revision`) may **pull** the template's current revision. A pull replaces the instance's QUESTIONS ONLY — the items with their frozen versions, points, order and milestones — and keeps everything else of the instance: title, dates, IP list, settings, grade scale, feedback and MCQ policies, duration and state; it then records the revision copied as `origin_revision` (a template that moved only in its settings is pulled as that record alone). It is allowed exactly where the item list is editable (F-EVAL-03): `draft` or `scheduled`, with no attempt of anybody, a teacher's own included (`409 items_frozen` / `409 locked` otherwise); a scheduled instance must still hold a question afterwards (`409 illegal_transition`, reason `no_items`). As for *Instantiate*, a question whose pool the course no longer links blocks it (`422 template_pool_unlinked` with the items) and a deprecated version only warns. The template must still be the instance's and a template of its course (`409 no_template` otherwise), at the revision the teacher confirmed (`409 template_moved`). It is offered where it would be accepted, and nowhere else: a **"template rev. N → M"** badge on the evaluation's row of the classroom's list, and a warning with its fix in the launch checklist (F-EVAL-23); both open a confirmation that lists the questions added, removed and changed (version, points, milestone), whether the order moves, and "rev. N → M", and says that the instance's own question changes are replaced — or, when no question differs, that only the revision is recorded. Audited as `template.pull` (ADR-031, addendum of 2026-09-28, PR B). | P2 | S |
| F-EVAL-27 | **Publish the correction** of an `exercise` that is running (`running` or `paused`), without closing it: an explicit, irreversible act of the teacher, from the live dashboard's overflow menu, behind a confirmation that says what the feedback policy will show. It opens the class debrief and its projection over the papers handed in so far (F-RES-03), counts as the release for the feedback policy on each student's results page (the key and the explanation per their options; nothing under `none`), lifts the score-only view between two attempts (F-EVAL-15), and grades every paper handed in from then on at hand-in; retakes continue, and no attempt is reopened any more (`409 correction_published`). Refused on an exam or a poll (`422 correction_not_allowed`) and in the waiting room or after the close (`409 correction_not_open`); a repeat answers the first instant. Audited as `evaluation.correction_publish` (ADR-050). | P2 | S |
| F-EVAL-28 | An `exam` accepts **trusted clients**: Safe Exam Browser (F-EVAL-17), an attested kiosk station (F-EVAL-29), or either; with neither, it is sat in the portal as before. A session opened by a trusted client reaches its exam only; opening a new one for the same student and exam ends the previous one, which the supervisor sees; the student's portal sessions are untouched (ADR-051 §1–4). | P2 | S |
| F-EVAL-29 | A **kiosk station** is a school Chromebook locked in a web kiosk on `/kiosk`. It attests itself to the platform through Chrome Verified Access, then shows its label, an 8-character code `XXXX-XXXX` (no ambiguous characters, 5 minutes, single use, renewed on expiry) and its QR code. The student, signed in on their phone, opens `/pair`, checks the station's label, picks one of the exams they can start now that accept the kiosk, and confirms; the station then opens that exam, confined to it. When the attempt ends (submit, close, expiry), the station's session ends and it returns to the pairing screen. A station never carries two sessions (ADR-051 §5, §7). | P2 | M |
| F-EVAL-30 | The **station registry** belongs to the admin: a station that attests for the first time appears as unnamed and cannot be paired until the admin names it; the admin may rename or retire a station (ADR-051 §5). | P2 | S |
| F-EVAL-31 | During a kiosk sitting the station **re-attests** every 10 minutes and before the submit. A refused attestation suspends the sitting (answers kept, writes refused) until the next accepted one; an attestation Google cannot answer suspends nothing. The supervisor sees an alert in both cases, and for every student the access mode (portal, SEB, or the station's label). From the dashboard, the supervisor may pair a station for a student without a phone, with the code the station shows (ADR-051 §6–8). | P2 | S |

## F-LIVE Live run

| Id | Requirement | Phase | Prio |
|---|---|---|---|
| F-LIVE-01 | The student sees on their home page the open evaluations of their classrooms and enters one in a single click. | P1 | M |
| F-LIVE-02 | In the waiting room they see the number of students present out of the number enrolled, as a progress ring, and the announced duration. | P1 | M |
| F-LIVE-03 | Before starting, the teacher sees the list of those present, those absent, and the students with extra time, and confirms. | P1 | M |
| F-LIVE-04 | The start is propagated to every client in under one second. | P1 | M |
| F-LIVE-05 | Every change to an answer is sent to the server with a batching delay of at most 300 ms, and acknowledged. The interface shows an unsaved or offline state. | P1 | M |
| F-LIVE-06 | After a reload or a reconnection, the student finds exactly the state of their answers and their position. | P1 | M |
| F-LIVE-07 | The countdown is computed on the server clock. The client corrects its offset on every tick. The server accepts a write until the deadline plus 3 seconds of grace, then refuses. | P1 | M |

## F-PROJ Projects

Graded work in a GitHub repository, one per student or per group: a lab as well as a semester project (ADR-035, heig-classroom's *assignment*; `docs/merge/03-github-projects.md`). A project is an **activity** of a classroom, beside its evaluations. It needs the classroom connected to an organization (F-GH-01) and Quiz's App installed there; without the App, nothing of this section shows. Its score is computed by the repository's own CI on the CI runners (ADR-007), never by `apps/runner`. Every member of a connected classroom's staff may create projects in Quiz, before the cutover too (D26 and its addendum of 2026-10-02); the import still brings heig-classroom's projects (F-PROJ-20). Until the project's student view (F-PROJ-15, merge task M3-09), the students see none of them.
