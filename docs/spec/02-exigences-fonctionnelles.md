# 2. Functional requirements

Every requirement is identified `F-AREA-nn`, with its original planning phase P1 / P2 / P3 and priority M / S / C (must / should / could). These labels are not implementation status; the [original phase plan](history/scope-planning.md#05-phases) is history. A requirement must be verifiable by a test or a demonstration.

## F-AUTH Authentication and accounts

| Id | Requirement | Phase | Prio |
|---|---|---|---|
| F-AUTH-01 | The user signs in through edu-ID with OpenID Connect. No local password. | P1 | M |
| F-AUTH-02 | On first sign-in, the account is created with name, email and a role derived from the edu-ID affiliation: a `staff` affiliation scoped to one of our institutions (`STAFF_AFFILIATION_DOMAINS`) gives `teacher`, unless a `student` affiliation of one of those institutions blocks it (a student elsewhere does not); anything else — another institution's `staff`, an unscoped `staff` — gives `student`. The default lists `heig-vd.ch` and `hes-so.ch`, because edu-ID releases HEIG-VD staff as `staff@hes-so.ch`; as an accepted consequence, the staff of the other HES-SO schools qualify too. The role is recomputed at every login by the same rule as any later change (`roles.ts`). | P1 | M |
| F-AUTH-03 | The admin is identified by a list of edu-ID identifiers in the configuration. They may promote or demote a user to `teacher`. | P1 | M |
| F-AUTH-04 | The session persists for 30 days. An explicit sign-out is available. A running exam never asks to sign in again. | P1 | M |
| F-AUTH-05 | A participant without an account may join an evaluation in `poll` mode through a session code, anonymously: no pseudonym is asked for nor stored (see 06 no. 18). Only an anonymous poll, which belongs to no classroom, takes such a participant; a classroom's poll admits its roster and its staff, signed in (ADR-014, addendum 2026-09-27). | P2 | M |

## F-ORG Courses, classrooms, rosters

| Id | Requirement | Phase | Prio |
|---|---|---|---|
| F-ORG-01 | A teacher creates a course with a name and a short code. They may add other teachers to it. | P1 | M |
| F-ORG-02 | A course references one or more pools. The course's evaluations draw from these pools. | P1 | M |
| F-ORG-03 | A teacher creates a classroom in a course with a name and a period: a free label and, optionally, a first and a last month (both or neither, the last not before the first). The form offers the current-or-next semester and the one after as presets and prefills the first; a preset fills the label only while it is empty or still the previous preset's name. The sidebar's flat list of classrooms shows those not archived, of a course the teacher has not hidden (F-ORG-11), whose period covers today (the browser's local date), with one month of margin on each side; an undated classroom always shows. The course cards, the course tree, the palette and the student home list every non-archived classroom. A classroom may be archived: it leaves the course list, the sidebar and the palette, and stays reachable from its course behind "Show archived". | P1 | M |
| F-ORG-04 | The roster is imported from CSV with at least the edu-ID email. Lines already known are merged, never duplicated. | P1 | M |
| F-ORG-05 | A student whose email appears in a roster is attached to the classroom on their first sign-in. | P1 | M |
| F-ORG-06 | Removed (ADR-053): no classroom join code; a student enters a roster only through the teacher (import or manual add) and is matched at sign-in. | — | — |
| F-ORG-07 | Every roster line carries an extra time in percent, 0 by default, and a free note. *It never applies to a project's deadline (D13): a project repository gets an individual deadline from its staff instead (F-PROJ-09, D13 as amended 2026-10-02).* | P1 | M |
| F-ORG-08 | The teacher may remove a student from a roster. Their past attempts are kept. | P1 | M |
| F-ORG-09 | Deleting a classroom deletes its evaluations, attempts, answers and gradings after a confirmation that names the classroom. The questions of the pool and the course's evaluation templates are not affected: a template belongs to the course and survives every classroom (ADR-031). Deleting a course deletes its templates, and its confirmation names how many. The classroom's GitHub link and its journal go with it: a journal in Quiz is deleted with its pages, revisions and assets, and the confirmation says so with the number of pages (when there are pages, the teacher types the classroom's name); for a journal in a GitHub repository only the copy goes, the repository is kept (F-JRN-04, ADR-057). Its projects go too, their rows only: no repository is ever deleted on GitHub, and the confirmation says so (F-PROJ-16, D19). | P1 | M |
| F-ORG-10 | A classroom may be duplicated to a new period, without roster nor attempts, with its evaluations as drafts. The copy's months are shifted by one semester (`shiftSemester` in `packages/domain`): a period that is exactly a semester becomes the next one (autumn 2026 → spring 2027), any other moves six months; an undated classroom stays undated. | P2 | S |
| F-ORG-11 | A teacher (or an admin) may hide a course they reach from their OWN navigation — the course list, both sections of the sidebar and the command palette — and show it again. Hiding is per user: the other staff members see nothing change, and nothing about the course changes either (classrooms, evaluations, pools). Pickers and the MCP `list_courses` keep listing a hidden course, and the course list brings hidden courses back behind "Show hidden" (#155, ADR-032, 06 no. 26). | P1 | S |
| F-ORG-12 | Every course has a **page of its own** (`/courses/:id`), reached from its name on the course list (card and table), from its row in the sidebar's course tree and from the command palette ("Go to the course"). It is in **tabs**, as the classroom's (F-ORG-13), each a path of its own (`/courses/:id/<tab>`): **Classrooms**, the default (the archived ones behind "Show archived"), **Templates** (F-EVAL-19), **Linked pools** (link and unlink), **Members** (the staff, each with their role — shown as Teacher (the stored role stays `owner`, ADR-068) or Assistant; product owner, 2026-10-10) and **Settings**. The Templates tab has an empty state that names the way to create one ("New template", "Save as template" in an evaluation's menu); the course list's card no longer lists templates, so the page is their one surface. The header's one primary action is the open tab's — New classroom, New template, Link a pool, Add a staff member — and Settings has none. Settings holds, in settings rows (D24), the name and the code (a code another course holds is refused, `409 duplicate_code`), hiding the course (F-ORG-11) and deleting it (F-ORG-09), which returns to the course list; the header shows the name and no longer edits it. Settings also holds the course's **Concepts** ("Notions"; [ADR-081, sixth addendum](../adr/ADR-081-vocabulaire-de-notions.md#sixth-addendum-2026-10-10-the-concepts-of-a-course-steps-7-9)): a plain list of the concepts the course teaches, sorted by label, edited with the question editor's concept picker (a `proposed` concept may be listed, and a new one created there). Only an owner replaces the list (`403 owner_required`); every member reads it (`GET /courses/:id/concepts`, also `concepts` in `GET /courses/:id`, so the MCP `get_course` and the teacher assistant read it); it is never part of a student payload. A concept a course lists cannot be deleted (`409 concept_in_use`), and a merge of concepts replaces it by the target in the course's list. Audited as `course.concepts_update`. Every staff member reaches the whole course (D04), with a role (ADR-068, product owner, 2026-10-04): an **owner** also adds and removes members and changes their roles, renames and deletes the course, links and unlinks its pools, creates and deletes its classrooms, and releases, withdraws and publishes the correction of its evaluations; an **assistant** does everything else, and is offered none of those actions (the server refuses them, `403 owner_required`). A new member is an assistant unless the Add-staff form says owner; an account already on the staff is refused (`409 already_staff`). The last owner can be neither removed nor made an assistant (`409 last_owner`), and the screens offer neither; any member may leave the course from their own row. An admin with Super Powers is an owner of every course. A course the caller does not reach reads as one that does not exist (ADR-031 addendum of 2026-09-28). | P1 | S |
| F-ORG-13 | The teacher's classroom page has a **Settings** tab, in settings rows (D24). It holds what the header held — rename, archive, delete (F-ORG-09) and the drill switch (F-DRILL-04) — plus a **GitHub** section (F-GH-02, F-GH-03) and a **Journal** section (F-JRN-02 to F-JRN-05), available whether the classroom is connected or not (a journal in Quiz needs no GitHub, ADR-057); the header keeps the name and the period. Its one accent is "Connect to GitHub" while the classroom is not connected, and nothing once it is: a classroom connected for its projects is not pushed towards a journal. A classroom that is never connected is a plain Quiz classroom, and nothing else of the page changes. | P2 | M |
| F-ORG-14 | The student's **Courses** entry (sidebar and bottom bar) is a route of its own that lists the student's classrooms — the classrooms where they hold a claimed seat, archived ones excepted — as cards; a card opens the classroom's page (F-ORG-15), and so does every classroom card of the student home (D07). **Activities** stays the summary of the active activities of every classroom, today's home: what is open now and what is coming, the latter grouped by day — Today, Tomorrow, This week, Later — by the calendar day each activity opens, in the browser's time zone ("This week" runs from the day after tomorrow to Sunday, weeks starting on Monday; an opening already passed is Today, an undated one Later), the soonest first in each, an empty day not drawn (product owner, 2026-10-01; no calendar view until the projects bring deadlines, D07). **Grades** (sidebar and bottom bar) is a route of its own, `/grades`: the student's finished work — what the home's "Past" listed, which the home no longer shows — grouped by classroom, the classroom of the newest row first, archived classrooms included and marked; per row the activity, its kind, its date, a status (released, grade not shared — released under the policy `none` —, results available, results pending, handed in, not taken), and the points and the Swiss grade where F-RES-04 lets the student read them; a "results available" row shows its points marked indicative, and no grade. A row opens the feedback page where it has something to show. No average there unless the teacher publishes the classroom's mean (F-GBOOK-05). A project row shows under its classroom like an evaluation's once its scores are released (F-PROJ-14). | P2 | M |
| F-ORG-15 | A student has a **page per classroom** at `/classrooms/:id`, the same address as the teacher's, which opens the student's page for a student, for a teacher in the student view (ADR-018) and in an impersonation session (ADR-034). Header: the classroom's name, the course's code and name, the period, the teachers, the student's time bonus. Its tabs are routes: **Activities** (by default) in three groups, Open now, Upcoming (grouped by day as on the home, F-ORG-14), Past, whose one accent is the button of the single most urgent open activity; **Journal** only when the classroom has one (F-JRN-07); **Groups** (`/classrooms/:id/groups`) only while a group set of the classroom reaches its students, where they form their groups (F-PROJ-22; product owner, 2026-10-05); **Grades**, the student's own cells of the classroom's gradebook (F-GBOOK-05). A group set open to the students is a row "Form your group until …" in Open now, here and on the home, leading to the Groups tab; it never takes the accent (F-PROJ-22, S3). A published project is listed in Activities like an evaluation, in the group its dates put it in (F-PROJ-04), and counts for the most urgent accent by its deadline. Empty state: nothing to do in this classroom right now. An archived classroom's page stays reachable by its address, as it is. A classroom where the caller is neither on the course's staff nor holding a claimed seat reads as one that does not exist (404). | P2 | M |
| F-ORG-16 | A course keeps a **catalog of conditions** (kind and text, as F-EVAL-33), in a section of its page's Settings tab, that the course's owners manage — add, edit, reorder, archive and restore — and its assistants read (product owner, 2026-10-09; ADR-079 §5); an entry is archived, never deleted. The conditions editor of an evaluation or a template offers the active entries of its course as checkboxes: ticking one copies its kind and text there, as a snapshot that later edits of the entry never change, and records which entry it came from; unticking removes that copy. A condition whose entry is archived, or belongs to another course, reads as a one-off condition. Neither the catalog nor that reference reaches a student view ([ADR-079](../adr/ADR-079-conditions-de-l-evaluation.md) §5, issue #584). | P2 | S |

## F-POOL Pools and organisation of questions

| Id | Requirement | Phase | Prio |
|---|---|---|---|
| F-POOL-01 | Every teacher has a private pool created automatically and may create others. The personal pool (`Polls`) is created on first use, by the first question the teacher keeps after a live poll (ADR-014, addenda item 6); a teacher who never keeps one never sees it. | P1 | M |
| F-POOL-02 | A pool contains hierarchical categories and questions. A question is in a single category or at the root. | P1 | M |
| F-POOL-03 | The list of questions can be filtered by type, concepts, difficulty, category, free text on the internal name and the statement. A typed `#word` resolves to every concept it may designate (either language, an alias, the qualified form) and filters on any of them; the address carries concept ids (amended by [ADR-081](../adr/ADR-081-vocabulaire-de-notions.md), third addendum §7). | P1 | M |
| F-POOL-04 | A question may be copied to another pool. The copy references its origin. | P1 | S |
| F-POOL-05 | A pool may be shared with other teachers with a `reader`, `contributor` or `owner` role. | P2 | S |
| F-POOL-06 | A public pool is readable by every teacher. Who writes in it is decided by its owner through members and linked courses ([ADR-013](../adr/ADR-013-partage-des-pools.md) §4, superseding "the admin designates its contributors"; open question 9). Publishing is the owner's switch (F-POOL-12), the catalogue and subscriptions are F-POOL-13. | P2 | S |
| F-POOL-07 | A pool exports as an archive of YAML files in the canonical format, one file per question, the category tree as folders. Importing this archive recreates the pool or merges into an existing pool by id. | P1 | S |
| F-POOL-08 | Import of GIFT and Moodle XML files for the supported types. Questions that cannot be converted are listed with the reason. | P2 | C |
| F-POOL-09 | Deleting a pool is refused while an evaluation or an evaluation template pins one of its question versions; the refusal names the holders the caller can open and counts the others (ADR-031). | P1 | M |
| F-POOL-10 | A teacher stars questions of any pool they can see — a reader and a public pool included — as personal favourites: a colleague never sees nor clears them, and nothing is audited. On the pool screen, a star on each row and card, Space on the focused one, "Star" in the bulk bar and "Clear favourites" (this pool, after a confirm that counts them). In the question picker of an evaluation or a template, the starred questions of the shown pool come first, and "Add favourites" adds every one that can be added, says what it skipped and why, then offers to unstar them. A star follows its question when it is moved to another pool; a copy starts without one; a soft-deleted question's star stays hidden until it is restored ([ADR-040](../adr/ADR-040-favoris-de-question.md)). | P1 | S |
| F-POOL-11 | **Report a question** (issue #680, level 1). Anyone who can read a question in the pool screen — a reader, a public reader, the staff of a linked course — reports a problem with it, with a message of at most 1 000 characters, from the question's menu; never a student nor a guest (a teacher route behind `poolAccess`, nothing of it reaches an attempt or `toStudent`). The pool's writers (owner, contributors) read every report of their pool; a reporter reads their own. A question with an open report shows an indicator on its row and in its header, beside (never merged with) the findings of the nightly review (ADR-060), and its Reports tab lists them. A writer — or an admin under Super Powers, which covers a public pool left without writers — resolves a report with an optional reply. Reports travel with a moved question (ADR-017); the open ones of a soft-deleted question close with it. Audited: `question.report`, `question.report_resolve`. Suggested edits are deferred. | P2 | M |
| F-POOL-12 | **Derived visibility, publication and description** (issue #680, lot 1; [ADR-013](../adr/ADR-013-partage-des-pools.md) decisions 8 and 9). The owner decides one thing, whether the pool is published (a switch; never the personal pool); the displayed visibility is derived: public if published, shared if it has a member or a linked course whose staff includes someone other than the owner, private otherwise. A public pool offers `contributor` and `owner` only when inviting. The card shows the visibility badge bottom-left, a description of at most three lines and, on a pool that is not yours, its owner beside the name. The owner writes a 280-character description in Settings and may accept an AI proposal; nothing is written without that act. Audited: `pool.publish`, `pool.unpublish`; an accepted description is a `pool.update`. | P2 | M |
| F-POOL-13 | **Public pool catalogue, subscriptions and read-only links** (issue #680, lot 2; [ADR-095](../adr/ADR-095-catalogue-des-banques-publiques.md)). "My pools" lists the pools a teacher owns, sits on, reaches through a linked course or subscribed to; a public pool they have none of those ties to is read and searched but not listed. The pools page has a catalogue panel of public pools only (never widened by Super Powers), searched over name, description, inferred domain and the concept labels (French and English) of the published questions, ranked by subscribers plus members, then use. Any teacher subscribes to a public pool (never a member row); the owner and the members read the subscribers by name, and cannot remove one; a public card shows a `users` counter. A course owner links a public pool to a course read-only without subscribing (its staff are readers; an `edit` link keeps making them contributors, and upgrading needs contributor access); `PUT /courses/:id/pools` carries each link's mode, keeps existing modes and lets the stronger win. Unpublishing first counts the subscribers, the read-linked courses and their templates that use the pool, then drops the read links and subscriptions, notifies each and revokes their streams. The pool's domain is a short bilingual label inferred from its concept labels on publication and nightly. Audited: `pool.subscribe`, `pool.unsubscribe`; `course.pools_update` records the modes. | P2 | L |

## F-EVAL Configuring an evaluation

| Id | Requirement | Phase | Prio |
|---|---|---|---|
| F-EVAL-01 | The teacher creates an evaluation in a classroom with a title, a mode `exam`, `exercise` or `poll`, and an ordered list of questions taken from the course's pools. The mode of an exam or an exercise stays editable until students are let in (F-EVAL-24, F-EVAL-25; [ADR-092](../adr/ADR-092-changer-le-mode-d-une-evaluation.md), 2026-10-09). | P1 | M |
| F-EVAL-02 | Every item carries a number of points. By default: 1 for the simple types, the number of test cases for code, the number of blanks for cloze. | P1 | M |
| F-EVAL-03 | Adding a question freezes its current published version. The evaluation shows when a more recent version exists and offers "Update" item by item or globally, as long as no attempt exists. | P1 | M |
| F-EVAL-04 | Time settings: `duration` per-student duration from their start, `deadline` common end time, `manual` closed by the teacher. The teacher answers one question, who drives the clock — **Scheduled** (« Planifiée »: the platform opens at `opens_at` and closes at `closes_at`, no waiting room) or **Live** (« En direct »: the teacher opens the waiting room, starts and closes; the date is a calendar hint only) — and one switch, a time limit per student, at most 8 hours (product owner, 2026-10-09); both are derived from and written to `timing` and `lobby`, never stored. A `manual` evaluation may carry an optional **safety deadline** in `closes_at`, which the ticker enforces like a common end; a `duration` attempt is cut at `closes_at` when there is one, even with time left. An `exam` must end by itself: `duration`, `deadline`, or `manual` with its safety deadline. `exercise` has a due date (amended by [ADR-086](../adr/ADR-086-planifiee-ou-en-direct.md), 2026-10-08). | P1 | M |
| F-EVAL-05 | The roster's extra time applies to `duration`; when a `closes_at` cuts the duration, it is added after the cut, so it carries the student past that end ([ADR-086](../adr/ADR-086-planifiee-ou-en-direct.md) §3). In `deadline` mode it extends the individual end beyond the common end, by a share of the **announced** window (`closes_at − opens_at` as the teacher set it, decision D8): time added to everybody during the run — an extension to all, a pause — moves the common end and never grows that share, for a late starter or a reopened attempt alike. The announced window follows the teacher's timing edits while the configuration is editable, and is frozen once it locks (F-EVAL-03) (#253). | P1 | M |
| F-EVAL-06 | Waiting room: `skip` the student starts alone, `auto` start when everybody present is there, `manual` the teacher starts. A Scheduled evaluation has none (`skip`); a Live one starts at `manual`, `auto` and (without a time limit) `skip` being advanced options ([ADR-086](../adr/ADR-086-planifiee-ou-en-direct.md)). | P1 | M |
| F-EVAL-07 | Navigation: `free`, `forward_only`, `milestones` with a flag per item beyond which there is no going back. | P1 | M |
| F-EVAL-08 | Presentation: `zen` one question per screen, `continuous` scrolling, `student_choice` only with `free` navigation. | P1 | M |
| F-EVAL-09 | Shuffling: question order yes / no, choice order yes / no for shuffleable questions. The order is derived from the attempt's seed and stable for a student. | P1 | M |
| F-EVAL-10 | Grade scale: grade = 1 + 5 × points / total, capped at 6, rounded to the tenth, configurable method: nearest by default. An item may be marked **bonus** in the builder: its points are left out of the total, so they can only lift a student (a score may read "21 / 18"); under negative marking its score is floored at 0; the flag is locked like the points, and an exam or exercise whose total is 0 is not opened (`no_graded_points`). The `threshold` scale is gone (ADR-052). | P1 | M |
| F-EVAL-11 | Feedback: `none`, `on_release` after the teacher releases the results, `immediate` once the student hands the attempt in (per hand-in, not per question: ADR-067, question 50), allowed for a poll or an exercise without a waiting room; refused for an exam or an exercise with an `auto` or `manual` waiting room (`isFeedbackAllowed`, open question 5, settled). The feedback includes or not the answer key and the explanation, two distinct options. A correction published while an exercise runs counts as the release for `on_release` (F-EVAL-27). | P1 | M |
| F-EVAL-12 | An evaluation may restrict IP addresses to a list of prefixes. The access code a student used to type is removed: a code on the board proves no presence ([ADR-053](../adr/ADR-053-retrait-des-codes-d-entree.md)); `access_code` is a poll's session code only (ADR-014). | P1 | S |
| F-EVAL-13 | An evaluation may enable the recommended full-screen mode and the **integrity journal** (`logVisibility`): leaving the page (tab hidden, window switched) is recorded; so is pasting a text of at least 20 characters not copied on the page (in the same tab), with the paste's length and whether the page had just lost the focus (derived by the server), never its content. On by default for an `exam`, off for an `exercise` (the teacher may switch it on); a poll never journals. The server stores nothing when it is off, nor from a delegated (impersonation) session; a `seb` or `kiosk` session journals like the portal. The student is told by the conditions (F-EVAL-33) and, outside SEB and the kiosk, by a toast when it happens (at most one per kind every five minutes); the teacher sees a discreet list (student, time, kind). Nothing is blocking, and the journal is a hint, never proof. It is deleted at the release of the grades, or six months after the close of an evaluation never released (N-DATA-03; amended by [ADR-088](../adr/ADR-088-journal-d-integrite.md), 2026-10-09). | P2 | S |
| F-EVAL-14 | An evaluation can be duplicated to the same classroom or to another one, provided the target course links the pools of all its questions (F-EVAL-01). | P1 | S |
| F-EVAL-15 | An `exercise` evaluation may allow several attempts, keeping the best or the last one (the teacher's choice), with an optional maximum. A retake starts blank, with a new question order, once the previous attempt is handed in and while the evaluation is open; the student sees the score of each attempt, not the correction, until the evaluation closes — or until the teacher publishes the correction (F-EVAL-27). The teacher may let a retake ask **only the questions to review** (`retakes.scope: to_review`, free navigation only, `422 retake_scope_navigation` otherwise): the questions at full marks in the latest attempt (or worth 0) are carried over read-only with their answer and validated grading into a new whole attempt, which counts against the maximum; between two attempts the student then also reads each question's standing — acquired, to review, awaiting correction — and nothing else of it, whatever the feedback policy, and chooses between **Redo the questions to review (n)** and **Redo everything** ([ADR-091](../adr/ADR-091-reprise-partielle-d-un-exercice.md), product owner, 2026-10-09). Results (their per-question statistics included), grade and CSV use the kept attempt; the pool's question statistics count exams only (F-STAT-01). An exam keeps one attempt (ADR-025). | P2 | C |
| F-EVAL-16 | An `exam` or `exercise` evaluation may use **negative marking**: every choice question is then scored so that a wrong answer costs points (single answer: +1 or −1/(n − 1); several: c/C − w/W, not floored) and no answer costs nothing. Per-question points may be negative; the evaluation total is floored at 0 and the grade computed from it. The student is told in the waiting room — among the conditions (F-EVAL-33) — and on each choice question. Frozen once an attempt exists (ADR-026, issue #130). | P1 | S |
| F-EVAL-17 | An `exam` may require **Safe Exam Browser**. ## F-JRN Journal

## F-PROJ Projects

Graded work in a GitHub repository, one per student or per group: a lab as well as a semester project (ADR-035, heig-classroom's *assignment*; `docs/merge/03-github-projects.md`). A project is an **activity** of a classroom, beside its evaluations. It needs the classroom connected to an organization (F-GH-01) and Quiz's App installed there; without the App, nothing of this section shows. Its score is computed by the repository's own CI on the CI runners (ADR-007), never by `apps/runner`. Every member of a connected classroom's staff may create projects in Quiz, before the cutover too (D26 and its addendum of 2026-10-02); the import still brings heig-classroom's projects (F-PROJ-20). Until the project's student view (F-PROJ-15, merge task M3-09), the students see none of them.

## F-GBOOK Gradebook

A classroom's grades in one table: its released evaluations and projects, and a mean (D06; F-RES-05 promoted). The gradebook reads the results; it never grades.

| Id | Requirement | Phase | Prio |
|---|---|---|---|
| F-GBOOK-01 | The teacher's classroom page has a **Grades** tab: one row per claimed student seat (a staff seat never appears, ADR-018 §3), one column per activity whose results are **released** — an evaluation (F-GRADE-09), a project (F-PROJ-14) —; the staff's table also lists the columns not released yet, where marks can be set, their grades not shown and out of the mean (ADR-074, product owner, 2026-10-05). Each cell is the Swiss grade to the tenth: for an evaluation what its results give. An **absence** is 1.0 shown with a sigil and a colour of its own (the school's `a1.0`), which counts as 1.0; it has two sources, both shown with the sigil — **derived** (a released **exam** not taken: a1.0, computed and never stored; an exercise not opened is an empty cell) and a **stored mark** the staff set (`absent`, or the teacher's own `score` out of a maximum, which also fills the cell of a student who never accepted a project, 06 no. 48) (ADR-074, product owner, 2026-10-05). A project not accepted is otherwise an empty cell, which does not count (06 no. 48). A mark wins over the grade beneath it, but replacing a real grade is deliberate (`override: true`, else `409 grade_exists`); clearing a mark is explicit; every set and clear is audited with before and after; a mark is staff-only until its column is released, and a student never reads its comment (ADR-074, product owner, 2026-10-05). Each cell names its source: the evaluation's results, or the project's teacher, review or frozen score (I42). An archived classroom's gradebook is read-only (`409 classroom_archived`; ADR-074, product owner, 2026-10-05). | P2 | M |
| F-GBOOK-02 | **Which columns count**: exams and projects by default, exercises when the teacher includes them, polls never. The **mean** of a student is the weighted mean of the columns that count and that have a grade for them, rounded to the tenth: Σ(wᵢ·gᵢ) / Σ(wᵢ) over the counted columns the student has a grade in (a column with no grade for them is skipped and the others renormalised; an absence counts as 1.0); 0 % keeps the column in view and out of the mean; all weights at 0 % give no mean (product owner, 2026-10-08). No ranking anywhere. | P2 | M |
| F-GBOOK-03 | A column follows its activity: a grade changed after the release (a regrade, a teacher score) changes the cell. | P2 | M |
| F-GBOOK-04 | **CSV export** of the table: e-mail, name, one column per activity, the mean; the format of F-RES-02; staff only, no audit (like F-RES-02). The administration has a Concepts tab, a queue of the concepts that are not merged, the `proposed` ones first, searchable and filtered by status. Each concept shows its labels, qualifiers and descriptions in both languages, who proposed it and when, and the instance-wide number of live questions that use it: a number only, which an administrator reads without Super Powers, while pool names and statements need them ([ADR-054](../adr/ADR-054-super-powers-admin.md), amended). Under them, **Ask the AI** (secondary, on demand, on the daily cap of the LLM gateway, purpose `concepts`) asks a model for further pairs from the labels and qualifiers alone — no description, alias, question, pool or person is sent; the pairs come back labelled as AI suggestions, filed as close, translation, homonym or related with a short reason, validated by the server, kept in the page only and never written automatically; Merge is offered for close and translation pairs only, Edit for all. Relations belong to the course concepts (#578). | P1 | M |
